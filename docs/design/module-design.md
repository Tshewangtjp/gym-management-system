# Gym Management System – Module Design

## 1. System Modules

### Module 1 – Authentication & User Management

- User registration
- User login/logout
- JWT authentication
- Role-based authorization
- Password management
- Profile management
- User/role administration

### Module 2 – Member Management

- Register members
- Update member information
- Delete members
- Search members
- View member profiles
- View membership status and validity
- Assign members to trainers

### Module 3 – Trainer Management

- Add trainers
- Update trainer information
- Delete trainers
- View trainer profiles
- Assign members to trainers
- View assigned members

### Module 4 – Membership Plan Management

- Create membership plans
- View membership plans
- Update membership plans
- Delete membership plans
- Configure pricing and duration
- Membership renewal
- Monitor membership validity

### Module 5 – Attendance Management

- Record daily attendance
- View attendance history
- View daily attendance
- Attendance statistics
- Attendance reports

### Module 6 – Payment Management

- Record membership payments
- Record renewal payments
- View payment history
- Track outstanding payments
- Payment status
- Revenue reports

### Module 7 – Dashboard & Reports

- Membership statistics
- Active/expired membership overview
- Daily attendance statistics
- Payment/revenue summaries
- Attendance reports
- Revenue reports
